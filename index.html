Process

How ChildSafe Alert Works

A simple 5-step process that reunites children with their families in minutes, not hours.

1
Report Missing
Guardian fills a quick form with child details & photo
2
Alert Generated
Unique Case ID & QR code created instantly
3
QR Shared
Alert shared with volunteers & displayed at checkpoints
4
Sightings Reported
Anyone scanning the QR can report a sighting instantly
5
Child Reunited ✓
Family notified & case closed with full timeline
